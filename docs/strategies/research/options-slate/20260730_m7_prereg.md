## 1. Parameterization: raw SVI (registered choice)

Fit **per `(root, session_date, expiry)`** the raw SVI form in total implied variance:

```
w(k) = a + b * ( rho * (k - m) + sqrt( (k - m)^2 + sigma^2 ) )
```

with `k = log(K / F)` and `w = iv^2 * T`.

**Why SVI and not a spline.** The entire purpose of M7 is trustworthiness at |delta| ~ 0.05, i.e. in the wings. A spline's behaviour in and beyond the sparse wings is governed by knot placement and end conditions, not by any financial constraint -- it can and will produce curvature artifacts exactly where OPT-047 reads it, and it has no defensible extrapolation. Raw SVI is **linear in `k` in both wings by construction**, which is the theoretically correct large-strike asymptotic (Lee's moment formula bounds the slope of total variance by 2), and its five parameters admit closed-form static-no-arbitrage constraints (Gatheral-Jacquier). A wing that is wrong under SVI is wrong by a bounded, inspectable slope; a wing that is wrong under a spline is unbounded. For a surface whose failure mode is "quietly extrapolates garbage at 0.05 delta", the parameterization with the rigid wing is the correct one.

**Cost of the choice, stated up front:** raw SVI cannot represent every observed smile (it has no independent control of the two wing slopes beyond `rho`), so it will show larger fit residuals than a spline on any given day. That is a feature here -- residual is measurable and reported; spline flexibility would hide misfit rather than remove it.

## 2. Forward and discounting: parity-implied forward, FRED discount

**Discount factor** `D = exp(-r*T)`: `r` from the repo's existing FRED reader
(`src/data/rates/fred_reader.get_fred_series`, series on disk at
`<storage>/alt_data/fred/`), **log-linearly interpolated across
`DGS1MO / DGS3MO / DGS6MO / DGS1 / DGS2` by maturity**, causal forward-fill as
that reader already implements. This is the repo's existing rate source; no new
source is introduced.

**Forward** `F`: from **put-call parity on the session's own quotes**, not from a
dividend model. For each strike with both a call and a put quote,
`F_k = K + (C - P) / D`; `F` is the **spread-weighted median** of `F_k` over the
paired strikes nearest the spot.

**This is how the "dividend-aware" requirement is met, and it is met at a higher
standard than the brief proposed.** The brief directed fetching a realized
dividend history from yfinance. Using *realized, later-paid* dividends to build
a forward as-of date `t` injects information not knowable at `t` -- for any
dividend not yet declared it is a straightforward lookahead leak, and the whole
chain's leakage discipline (spec v2 Section 1.3) forbids it. The parity forward
embeds **the market's own dividend expectation as priced at the snapshot
minute**, is point-in-time by construction, needs no external dividend series,
and is what dealers actually use. **No yfinance dividend wrapper is added.**

**Divergence recorded.** Realized dividends are still useful as an *ex-post
validation* of the implied forward, and the implied dividend yield
`q = r - log(F/S)/T` is reported as a diagnostic. It is never an input.

## 3. Fit inputs (registered filters -- no others)

Per expiry, a quote enters the fit iff all hold:
1. **OTM only**: calls with `K >= F`, puts with `K < F`. (ITM premium is dominated by intrinsic; IV is not numerically identified there.)
2. `mid >= 0.05`. Penny options carry no identified IV -- a 0.01/0.02 market is a 67%-wide spread.
3. Black-76 inversion of the mid against `(F, D, T)` succeeds and returns `iv` in `(0.01, 5.0)`.
4. `iv_shipped == 0.5` exactly is **not** a filter -- the shipped IV is not an input to M7 at all. M7 re-derives IV from quote mids. The 0.5 solver sentinel therefore cannot contaminate the fit.

**Weights**: `1 / max(iv_ask - iv_bid, 0.005)` -- each point is trusted in
proportion to how tightly its own quote pins its IV. This downweights the noisy
far wing without discarding it.

## 4. Refusal rules (registered). Null + reason code, never a silent number.

An expiry is refused, `iv_smooth = NULL`, with one of:

| reason code | condition |
|---|---|
| `OK` | fitted |
| `ZERO_DTE` | `dte == 0` (total variance degenerate, delta is a step) |
| `DTE_OUT_OF_RANGE` | `dte > 400` |
| `NO_FORWARD` | fewer than **4** paired call/put strikes |
| `IMPLAUSIBLE_FORWARD` | implied `q = r - log(F/S)/T` outside `[-0.10, +0.15]` |
| `TOO_FEW_STRIKES` | fewer than **8** usable OTM points |
| `ONE_SIDED` | fewer than **3** usable points on either side of `k = 0` |
| `FIT_FAILED` | optimizer did not converge |
| `ARB_VIOLATION` | fitted params violate the static no-arb constraint set |

Per-contract rows additionally carry `extrapolated = true` where the contract's
`k` lies outside the fitted `k` range. Flagged, not withheld.

Refusal thresholds are registered blind. **If the resulting refusal rate is
high, it is reported as-is; the thresholds are not tuned to reduce it.**

## 5. No-arbitrage constraint set

Imposed as bounds/penalty during the fit and re-checked after:
- `b >= 0`, `|rho| < 1`, `sigma > 0`
- `a + b*sigma*sqrt(1 - rho^2) >= 0` (total variance non-negative everywhere)
- `b*(1 + |rho|) <= 4/T` (wing slope bound; no butterfly explosion)

Butterfly (Gatheral `g(k) >= 0`, i.e. non-negative risk-neutral density) and
calendar (total variance non-decreasing in `T` at fixed `k`) are **measured and
reported as violation rates by bucket, never silently repaired.**

## 6. D-047/030 integrity gate -- operationalized here, BEFORE measuring

The registered gate text is *"if the surface diverges materially at 0.05 delta,
both 047 and 030 need a different mark source"*. **"Diverges materially" is
undefined in the doc chain** (confirmed by direct search). I fix it now, blind,
following the D-043 / D-048 precedent in the wave-0 doc:

**Sample:** SPY and QQQ; expiries with `dte in [21, 45]`; per session the
contract whose *smoothed* `|delta|` is nearest `0.05`, both rights; a seeded
random sample of **200 sessions per root** (`seed = 20260730`).

**PASS iff both hold, per root:**
- **(a)** `iv_smooth` lies within the raw quote's own `[IV(bid), IV(ask)]` band in **>= 90%** of sampled contract-sessions;
- **(b)** median `|iv_smooth - IV(mid)|` **<= 1.5 vol points** (0.015 absolute).

Reported descriptively but **not** gating: p90/p99 of `|iv_smooth - IV(mid)|`,
signed bias, and relative price divergence `|P_smooth - P_mid| / P_mid`.

Criterion (a) is the economically meaningful one -- "is the surface a mark a
desk could actually use" is exactly "is it inside the market". (b) guards
against a wide-quote regime where (a) passes vacuously.

**This is a data-integrity gate. It consumes no trials and produces no P&L.**

## 7. Scope

`options_chain_eod` exists only for **SPY 2017-01..2025-12 (108 partitions)** and
**QQQ 2012-06..2025-12 (162, less the real 2015-11 hole)**. M7 is built over
exactly that. Single-name roots are out of scope and would additionally require
the unbuilt V7 split-adjustment layer (strikes are AS-REPORTED, with
non-standard deliverables) before any surface could be fitted across a split.
